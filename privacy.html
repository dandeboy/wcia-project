Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 03, 2025

Thank you for your interest in joining forces with others to reduce Africa's risk of experiencing ever-widening disparity among its children. At the World's Children in Africa (WCiA) Project — a child-focused social investment CSR initiative of Net-Afrique Multiservices — we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled responsibly.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, https://wciaproject.org (the "Site"). Whether you are a partner, supporter, volunteer, or visitor, your privacy is of utmost importance to us. Our practices are designed to align with our mission: to empower the children of today and tomorrow by breaking cycles of poverty and inequity and unlocking Africa's vast potential.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

When you interact with our Site, you may voluntarily provide personal information such as:

  • Your name and contact details (e.g., email address, phone number)
  • Information you provide through contact forms or subscription sign-ups
  • Any additional details you choose to share to support our mission

2.2 Non-Personal Information

We automatically collect certain non-personal information when you access our Site. This may include:

  • Browser type and version
  • Device information and operating system
  • IP address, referring/exit pages, and URLs
  • Usage data, including pages visited and time spent on the Site

This information is used for statistical analysis, system administration, and to help us improve the quality of our Site.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected for purposes that include, but are not limited to:

  • Enhancing User Experience: Improving content, functionality, and ensuring the Site operates effectively.
  • Communication: Sending newsletters, updates, and information related to the WCiA Project and our initiatives.
  • Mission Advancement: Helping coordinate partnerships, volunteer efforts, and projects that drive our social investment goals.
  • Compliance and Security: Maintaining the security of our Site and protecting against misuse or unauthorized access.

4. Disclosure of Information

Your information may be shared in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We may engage trusted third-party service providers to support our operations (e.g., website hosting, data analysis). These providers are obligated to ensure the confidentiality of your information.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, or in response to a lawful request by a public authority.
  • Protection of Rights: In cases where disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of WCiA Project, our users, or others, we may reveal information as needed.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure that your data is protected against unauthorized or unlawful processing and against accidental loss, destruction, or damage. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to secure your information, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including:

  • The right to access and obtain a copy of your data.
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate data.
  • The right to request the deletion of your data (subject to certain restrictions).
  • The right to restrict or object to the processing of your data.

If you wish to exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our Site may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience, analyze usage, and support our communication efforts. Most web browsers automatically accept cookies, but you may adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies if you prefer. Note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of the Site.

8. Third-Party Websites

Our Site may contain links to third-party websites. Please note that we are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies when you visit them.

9. Children's Privacy

Although the WCiA Project is dedicated to the welfare and empowerment of Africa's children, our Site is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under this age threshold. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately so we can take steps to remove the information.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date. We encourage you to review this Policy periodically to stay informed about how we are protecting your information.

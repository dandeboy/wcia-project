WHO WE ARE

Our Mission & Values

Driven by a deep belief that every child in Africa deserves a fair chance at life, education, and opportunity.

OUR MISSION

The World's Children in Africa (WCiA) Project exists to mobilise global social investment towards the welfare, education, and empowerment of children across Africa. We believe that investing in Africa's children today is the single most powerful lever for transforming the continent tomorrow — breaking centuries-old cycles of poverty, inequity, and underdevelopment.

"To grow Africa tomorrow is to prioritize investments in its children today."

OUR VISION

A continent where every child — regardless of geography, gender, or circumstance — has access to quality education, healthcare, nutrition, and a safe environment in which to thrive and realise their full potential.

WHAT GUIDES US

Our Core Values

Child-First

Every decision, programme, and partnership is evaluated through the lens of its impact on children.

Integrity

We operate with full transparency and accountability to our donors, partners, and the communities we serve.

Collaboration

We believe the most lasting change is achieved when governments, businesses, and civil society act together.

Sustainability

Our programmes are designed to create self-reinforcing cycles of progress that outlast any single intervention.

Equity

We prioritise the most marginalised and underserved children, ensuring no child is left behind.

Measurable Impact

We hold ourselves to rigorous standards of evidence, continuously measuring and improving our outcomes.

HOW WE WORK

Our Approach

01

CSR-Driven Social Investment

WCiA is a cause-related CSR initiative of Net-Afrique Multiservices. A portion of every product purchase and partnership agreement is channelled directly into child-welfare programmes across Africa.

02

Donors' Roundtable

We convene a formidable network of donors — individuals, corporations, and foundations — whose collective energy and resources produce transformative, large-scale impact for children.

03

Alignment with Global Frameworks

Our work is aligned with UNICEF's mandate and the UN Sustainable Development Goals, ensuring our programmes contribute to internationally recognised standards for child welfare.

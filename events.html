About Our Events

Sensitizing and mobilizing investment for Africa's children.

UNICEF we know, is working in over 190 countries and territories to protect the rights of every child, by reaching to the most disadvantaged children and adolescents everywhere. Several other child-focused organizations are as well doing same.

With the UNICEF report of an anticipated huge shift in the world's child population towards Africa – with its potential to undermine the eradication of poverty and the marginalisation of many children, it is important that a multi polar world needs be led by a multiplicity of institutions, in this regard.

Considering the landscape of charity and philanthropy in international development and humanitarian assistance, WCiA is poised to lend a hand, from time to time, through events, to sensitize and mobilize investments to help mitigate the consequences of the huge shift in the world's child population towards Africa, and its negative potentials.

Ideally, the WCiA project initiative is a response to the United Nations call for private sector participation towards the realization of the sustainable development goals initiative in Africa.

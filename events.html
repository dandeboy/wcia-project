Our Poise

Because it is important that a multi polar world needs be led by a multiplicity of institutions; and considering the landscape of charity and philanthropy in international development and humanitarian assistance, WCiA is poised to lend a hand, from time to time, through events, to sensitize and mobilize investments to help mitigate the consequences of the huge shift in the world's child population towards Africa, and its negative potentials, as a response to the United Nations call for private sector participation towards the realization of the sustainable development goals initiative in Africa.

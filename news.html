News Brief: The Headlines

Updates from across WCiA's programmes, partnerships, and the classrooms we support.

JULY 2026

WCiA Project To Distribute Children's Writing Companion To Schools in Uyo

The World's Children in Africa (WCiA) Project is set to roll out a fresh distribution of the Children's Writing Companion — its flagship cause-related gift product — to primary schools across Uyo in Akwa Ibom State. The exercise forms part of WCiA's commitment to placing developmental learning materials directly into the hands of children who need them most.

Thus, the WCiA Project is inviting individuals and corporate organizations to partner in this child-focused social investment initiative to support this and future distribution drives. Updates on participating entities shall be published timely as the exercise progresses.

JUNE 2026

WCiA Project Set To Welcome Three New Corporate Partners To Its Donors RoundTable

WCiA's Donors' RoundTable — the Project's formidable network of individual and corporate partners committed to Africa's growth, future, and children — is set to welcome three new corporate partners into its fold. The expansion reflects a growing recognition among the private sector of the urgent need for sustained investment in the welfare, education, and empowerment of vulnerable children across the continent.

Organisations and individuals interested in joining the Donors' RoundTable are encouraged to reach out through WCiA's partnership channels to learn how they can contribute.

A Typical WCiA Project-Supported Classroom, Loading…

WCiA's team is preparing a closer look inside one of the classrooms benefiting from the Project's ongoing support — a field feature capturing what an ideal classroom should look like.

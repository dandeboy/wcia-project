About Our Cause-Related Product

The Children's Writing Companion — for world's children in Africa.

🎁1
The Product

Consequent to the child-focused investment objective of the World's Children in Africa (WCiA) Project, is this cause-related gift product – the "Children's Writing Companion – for world's children in Africa".

📖2
What It Is

The product is an educative writing material, loaded with health, education, child protection & other child development messages (with pictures), to help prepare the children to be well receptive of child-focused programs targeted towards their development by humanitarian service delivery agencies and organizations.

🌍3
What's More

This product avails individuals and corporate entities the opportunity to participate and help provide the much needed support to the poor and vulnerable world's children in Africa; and also enable them participate in the United Nations' Sustainable Development Goals (SDG) initiative in Africa.

✅4
Ideal for Donation/Gifting To
  • Children between ages 4–16
  • Schools
  • Child Care Providers
  • NGOs
  • Parents/Guardians
  • Teachers/Instructors
⭐5
It's Impact

A gift of this companion to a child in Africa, will enable that child become enlightened and better oriented to not becoming a casualty of some critical health, education, and other disaster that can hinder his/her integration, survival and development.

How This Product Works

A gift of this Children's Companion, to any one child, goes beyond being just a book gift.

01

You Buy a Product

Proceeds from every subscription/purchase of the Companion are donated to select humanitarian service delivery agencies and organizations providing critical child-focused programs targeted at the poor and vulnerable world's children in Africa.

02

A Great Portion Goes to Children

The more subscriptions this companion gets, the more funding is made available to these delivery agencies; and this means the provision of more child-focused programs to the poor and vulnerable world's children in Africa.

03

Casualties Are Prevented

The more children reached by the child-focused activities of the select humanitarian organizations, means the emancipation of more children from becoming casualties.

04

Real Impact Is Created

The more children emancipated, the more we help secure Africa's future by availing her children the opportunity to realize their rights and potentials and contribute to Africa's socio-economic and political growth.

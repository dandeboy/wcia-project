Ways to Partner & Support

Thank you for your interest in joining others to help Africa reap tomorrow the benefits of her today's children.

You can support the critical health, education, and other development needs of the millions of poor and vulnerable children in Africa in any of the following ways. Support in one or more ways is always welcome.

1

Media Partnership

For journalists and all kinds of media service providers who want to help tell the story and extend our reach.

2

Professional Partnership

Enlist as a professional partner in health, education, or any other discipline relevant to this initiative.

3

Operational Items

Provide operational support — project monitoring and distribution vehicles, office equipment, health and educational materials, and similar in-kind gifts.

4

Cash Donations

Make a direct financial contribution towards our programmes — every naira, dollar, or pound counts.

5

Volunteering

Enlist as a volunteer to assist the project in your country or region.

6

Distribution Partnership

Enlist as a distributor for the Children's Writing Companion and other donated materials and equipment across Africa.
